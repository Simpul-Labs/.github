## 🧩 About Us

**Simpul Labs** is a technology-focused lab that connects ideas, people, infrastructure, and software into practical digital solutions.

Our work covers the journey from **an initial idea → design → development → deployment → monitoring → maintenance**.

The word **"Simpul"** represents a connection point — a place where different components come together to form something useful.

> **Connecting Ideas into Digital Solutions.**
